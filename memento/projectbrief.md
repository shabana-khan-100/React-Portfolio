# Project Brief: Personal Portfolio for Shabana Khan

## 1. Objective

The primary objective of this project is to design and develop a professional online portfolio for Shabana Khan. This portfolio will serve as a dynamic and interactive resume, effectively showcasing her skills, work experience, and key projects to potential employers, recruiters, and clients.

## 2. Core Requirements

The portfolio must meet the following core requirements:

- **Responsive Design**: The website must be fully responsive and accessible on all devices, including desktops, tablets, and mobile phones.
- **Single-Page Application (SPA)**: To ensure a seamless and fast user experience, the portfolio will be built as a single-page application.
- **Key Sections**: The portfolio will include the following sections:
  - **Professional Summary**: A concise and compelling summary of Shabana's professional background.
  - **Skills**: A comprehensive list of her technical skills, categorized for clarity.
  - **Work Experience**: A detailed overview of her professional roles and responsibilities.
  - **Key Projects**: A showcase of her most significant projects, with descriptions and technologies used.
  - **Education & Certifications**: A summary of her academic qualifications and professional certifications.
  - **Contact Form**: A functional contact form to allow visitors to get in touch with her.
- **Modern UI/UX**: The portfolio will feature a clean, modern, and visually appealing design that reflects her professionalism.
