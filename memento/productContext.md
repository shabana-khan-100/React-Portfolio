# Product Context: Shabana Khan's Personal Portfolio

## 1. Problem Solved

A traditional resume, while informative, is a static document that often fails to capture the full scope of a developer's capabilities. This project addresses that limitation by creating a dynamic and interactive online portfolio.

The portfolio will:

- **Provide a Richer Narrative**: Go beyond bullet points to tell a compelling story about Shabana's professional journey and technical expertise.
- **Showcase, Not Just Tell**: Allow her to demonstrate her skills through live project examples and a well-crafted personal website.
- **Enhance Professional Brand**: Establish a strong online presence that reinforces her brand as a skilled and modern full-stack developer.

## 2. User Experience (UX) Goals

The portfolio is designed with the following UX goals in mind:

- **Clarity and Readability**: The content will be well-organized and easy to scan, allowing visitors to quickly find the information they need.
- **Intuitive Navigation**: A clear and simple navigation structure will guide users through the different sections of the portfolio.
- **Modern and Clean Aesthetic**: The design will be professional, modern, and visually appealing, creating a positive impression.
- **Performance**: The site will be optimized for fast loading times to ensure a smooth and enjoyable user experience.
- **Responsiveness**: The layout will adapt seamlessly to different screen sizes, providing an optimal viewing experience on any device.
